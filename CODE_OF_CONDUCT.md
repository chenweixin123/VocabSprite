# Code of conduct

We collaborate with rigor and respect. Be specific about evidence, assumptions,
and uncertainty; critique work rather than people; welcome accessibility needs;
and do not publish another person's private study data or security details.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, deliberate misinformation, and license or
privacy violations are not accepted. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions
and restrict participation to protect the community. Report conduct concerns by
the same private route described in `SECURITY.md`; a public release must replace
that temporary route with a concrete contact.
